What it does

From “pet shop in Tbilisi” to a list you can call.

Any business, any city

Type what you're looking for, in English and Georgian, and where. Bizfinder asks the map service and sets aside the loosely related places maps pad their answers with, like malls in a pet-shop search, on a sheet of their own.

The whole city, box by box

Google returns at most 60 places per search. Bizfinder can split a big city into smaller areas and search each one, so you get hundreds.

Emails and social pages

It visits each business's own website and collects email addresses and links to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp and Telegram.

See who needs a website

Every business is sorted by web presence. One click shows only the ones with no site, just a social page, or a site that's down.

A spreadsheet to keep

Each search is saved as an Excel file with a Summary sheet and charts, or as CSV, ready to open in Google Sheets.

A call list that remembers

Log how each call went, how you got in touch, what they said and what to say better next time. Notes save as you type, every call is kept with its date, and the next business is one key away.

How it works

Three steps, a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Search

    Pick Google Maps or TomTom, type the kind of business and the city, and press Start.

  2. 2

    Collect

    Bizfinder finds the businesses, then visits their websites. You watch it happen live.

  3. 3

    Call

    Choose “Needs a website” and “Not called yet”, open the first one, and work down the list.
